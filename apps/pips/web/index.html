The Morning Paper

Pips

An always-on-top desktop widget that turns your day into a score.

What it does

  • Pomodoro timer — work to short break to long break, auto-starting the next session.
  • Soundscapes — rain, thunder, brown noise, cafe and more, generated locally.
  • Live app tracking — minutes per app, sampled from the foreground window.
  • Daily score — a 1–5 star rating, with goals, streaks and XP.
  • Coach & reminders — daily and weekly reports, plus stand-up / stretch / water and 20-20-20 eye nudges.

Run it

  • Double-click Launch Widget.bat, or run pythonw app.py
  • Copy config.example.json to config.json and edit as needed
